dhompet is an intuitive, secure, and 100% offline personal finance and expense management app.

Designed to help you stay on top of your daily finances with zero hassle, no annoying ads, and complete privacy peace of mind.

Key Features of dhompet:
- 100% Offline & Private: All transaction data is stored locally on your device.
- Simple Expense & Income Tracking: Log your transactions in seconds with a clean, modern UI.
- Multiple Accounts: Manage cash wallets, bank accounts, and digital cards with ease.
- Custom Categories: Personalize categories with custom icons and color palettes.
- Budget Planning: Set category and total monthly budgets to keep your spending disciplined.
- Rich Reports & Analytics: Gain deep insights with interactive cashflow and spending charts.
- Loans & Debts Tracker: Manage borrowed and lent money with clear due dates.
- Easy Backup & Restore: Export and import your data anytime for hassle-free device switching.
- Elegant Dark and Light themes.

Take control of your financial journey today with dhompet!
